# Verification of Section 18 (vapor)

An independent verifier reviewed the finished scene from rendered frames. Their findings follow. The director has read them and added rulings; where a ruling differs from the art brief or from a suggested fix, the ruling wins.

## Verdict

Nearly ready to show. The skip works exactly as briefed: byte-identical repeats, a stuttering counter and a clean jump at 4.0. The title, crossings, coverage, purity and speed all pass. The most important fix is the busts: they are nearly the pelican's pale pink and their heads line up behind its head at u 1.35, 4.2 and 5.3, so they should be shaded darker and more faceted to read as coloured stone and as classical busts rather than pawn-like lumps.

## The director's rulings

1. **The busts are coloured stone, clearly darker than the pelican.** Shade them in mid tones: orchid and violet planes, a pink key light only as narrow planes on the lit side, a cyan rim on the far side, deep violet in the hollows. No facet paler than about `#d79be8`. The pelican's body stays the palest thing in the picture after the sun and the title window. Check the three passing moments (u = 1.35, 4.2, 5.3) at full size: the pelican must read at a glance and the bust must read as something behind it.
2. **Busts 2 and 3 stand a little taller** (top of the head at about y = 538, on taller column stumps), so that when the pelican passes, the bust's face is seen above the pelican's head, looking over it, and its hair no longer sits behind the pelican's crest. Bust 1 keeps its height (the title window is above it).
3. **Model the busts as busts.** In profile each needs a chest with depth: a shoulder line that slopes from the neck, a cut arm plane, a flat cut base; the head about 15 % larger. On the faces, make brow, nose ridge, lips and chin separate planes that catch the key light, so a front view is a face and not a dark patch. Fewer, larger hair facets. Look at all fifteen views large and at film scale.
4. **The pelican's legs** are warm: peach to orange (`#ffb07a`), with the deep violet outline, so they read against the cyan tubes and the grid. A foot plainly on each pedal.
5. **The sun shows its five bars.** Raise it (centre about y = 572, radius about 90) or lower the range in front of it, so that five dark bars, thickening downward, are seen across its lower half above the mountains. It stays about 150 units right of the middle of the view, never behind the pelican.
6. **Title window:** 250 units wide (x 160 to 410), so it is whole for a little over two seconds. Its own caption bar holds no text, only the close box, so the tape's PLAY and counter never cross lettering. The three title lines stay below y = 466 at their present sizes.
7. **Palms:** long, clean fronds of a few large facets that droop, in place of the crumpled shards. No trunk within 30 units of a bust at any time the pelican is passing that bust.
8. Left as they are: bust 2 stepping two views at u = 4.0 (it follows from the skip), the faint reflection.
9. Keep everything the verifier confirmed: `tape(u)` and the byte-identical skip frames, views changing only on beats, the counter, the crossings, purity, speed.

## Findings

### [must-fix] Busts are the same pale pink as the pelican and merge with it where it passes

Evidence: The brief requires busts 'never the same pale tone as the pelican'. At u 1.35 (108.35), 4.2 (111.2) and 5.3 (112.3) the bust's chest and face are pale pink-white, close to the pelican's body. At 4.2 and 5.3 the bust's head sits directly behind the pelican's head, so its hair reads as a crest growing from the pelican. Only the violet outline separates them.

Suggested fix: Shade the busts darker and more saturated: violet or magenta shadow facets, a cyan right side, and no facet lighter than about 75% value where it can sit behind the pelican. Or nudge the bust x positions or head heights a few units so the bust head never lines up with the pelican's head at the passing frames.

### [should-fix] Busts read as balusters or chess pawns in profile, and front faces are muddy

Evidence: Crops of 110.05 and 110.95: in profile each chest is a round bowl on a waisted block with no shoulder line, and the head is small for it. Front views (bust 1 at 108.55, bust 3 at 112.75) show the face as a dark smudge with no clear brow, nose or mouth facets. The hair is noisy speckle rather than faceted curls.

Suggested fix: Give the profile views a chest with depth: a sloping shoulder line front to back and a cut-off arm plane. Enlarge the head about 15%. Lighten the face's front facets so the nose ridge and brow catch the pink key light. Reduce curl count in favour of larger, clearer facets.

### [should-fix] Pelican's cyan legs disappear against the cyan bike tubes and grid

Evidence: Zoom of 108.95: the legs are thin cyan strokes the same hue as the down tube and the grid lines. Only a cyan foot on the pedal is readable, so the pedalling legs the guide requires hardly read.

Suggested fix: Give the legs a darker teal or violet outline and a two-tone shade, or move them to a peach or pink that contrasts with the cyan frame. Keep the 1.3-unit violet outline on them too.

### [should-fix] Barred sun barely reads: two thin bars, lower half hidden by mountains

Evidence: Frames 110.0, 111.0 and the still: only two thin dark bars cross the sun before the mountain range covers the rest. The brief asks for five bars thickening downward across the lower half, the signature of the style.

Suggested fix: Raise the sun (centre about y 585), lower or thin the mountains in front of it, or start the bars nearer the sun's equator, so all five graded bars show above the range.

### [minor] Title is in full view for only about 2.0 s, and the tape overlay crosses its title bar

Evidence: The window's right edge is still clipped at u 0.65 and fully in at 0.75; the left edge is in at 2.65 and clipped at 2.75. That is about u 0.70–2.70, exactly the 2 s minimum. The counter covers the title bar and close box from about u 0.5 to 1.6 (107.65, 108.5). PLAY covers 'sunset.bmp' from about u 2.6 to 3.0 (109.65, 110.0).

Suggested fix: Narrow the window to about 250 units for some margin, or accept it. Drop the overlay slightly lower or let the window's title bar sit below y 462 so PLAY and the counter pass over sky, not chrome.

### [minor] Bust 2 jumps two views at u = 4.0

Evidence: Bust 2 is in left profile through the skip (110.05, 110.95) and is front-on at 111.0, skipping the three-quarter view. This follows from the lost 3.5 beat and is noted in As built, but it makes the turn look like a jump.

Suggested fix: Optional: let it reach the three-quarter view on the 3.0 beat (before the skip) so the step at 4.0 is only one view.

### [minor] Palm crowns read as crumpled shards; a palm trunk touches bust 3 at the exit

Evidence: Every frame: the fronds are jagged zig-zag spikes rather than long triangular leaves. At 113.0 the trunk at x 815 sits against bust 3's left shoulder.

Suggested fix: Use longer, cleaner fronds with fewer, larger facets that droop. Move that palm about 15 units left, or bust 3 right, for clear air.
