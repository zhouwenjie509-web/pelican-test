# Verification of Section 8 (mughal)

An independent verifier reviewed the finished scene from rendered frames. Their findings follow. The director has read them and added rulings; where a ruling differs from the art brief, the ruling wins.

## Verdict

Not ready to show yet. Timing, crossings (wheel bottoms match within 2 px at u = 0), coverage, purity and the half-size render are all sound, and the page reads convincingly as a Mughal album page. The single most important fix is the bar-2/bar-3 payoff. Make the astonishment readable at film scale, with heads turned and the balcony guests really looking down at the channel, and make the top-view pelican legibly a cyclist from above. Right now it reads as a flattened bird, and the joke does not land. The undersized English and date lines in the title must also be enlarged to the 30 px minimum.

## The director's rulings

1. Balcony party: in the astonished state all three tilt their heads down about 35 degrees, chins over the rail, eyes lowered to the water channel. At ease they may look out over the garden.
2. Title type: the guide's sizes are font sizes, but the result must also be easy to read. Raise every line that is not the Chinese name until its capital letters and digits are at least 26 px tall on screen at zoom 1.1 (about 12.5 units of font size for most faces; measure it on a full-size frame). The Chinese name stays at least 44 px. A title fixed in the world stays within 270 units of width and in full view for at least 2.1 s. Make the panel an illuminated cartouche: a gold ground with lobed cloud-band reserves that follow the words, a fine gold scroll border and small flowers in the gaps. Shift it right by up to 20 units if that is needed for the view time.
3. Top view in the channel: it must read at film scale as a cyclist seen from above. Show the bicycle's whole line (dark tyre bands ahead of and behind the body, the handlebar across in front of the breast), a short visible neck and a head with the bill reaching forward over the front wheel, and a foot on each pedal that visibly alternates left and right with the crank. Remove the long pink thigh bars.
4. The astonishment at u = 3.0 must be visible on the contact sheet, not only in a crop: heads turned towards the pelican or the channel, a raised forearm in a skin tone that stands out from the robe, the lute lowered. The switch itself stays exactly at u = 3.0 with no fade longer than 0.08 s.
5. Gallery still: choose u and cx so the bill does not cross a face and at least two raised fingers are plainly visible.
6. Minor: thin the pale halo round the side-view pelican and bicycle to a hairline, as long as the pelican still stands clear of the garden.

## Findings

### [must-fix] Balcony guests do not look straight down at the channel

Evidence: Balcony crop at 52.75 (u = 5.75) and 50.02: all three balcony figures hold their heads level in profile with their eyes forward. The man in blue faces right while the other two face left, so none looks down at the water. The brief says the balcony party 'look straight down at what the channel shows', and As built claims it does. Their facing also ignores where the pelican is: at u = 5.75 the pelican is to their right, but the woman and the man in yellow face left, away from it.

Suggested fix: Repaint the three balcony heads in state 1 tilted down about 30-40 degrees, with lowered eyes and chins over the rail, all aimed down toward the channel. That way the bar-3 payoff (they are looking at the top view) reads.

### [must-fix] English and date lines of the title are below the 30 px readability minimum

Evidence: Pixel measurement on 50.02 (zoom 1.1): the cap height of 'Mughal' is about 21 px and the full line is 38 px. The red date digits '1600' run from y 193 to 212 (about 20 px), and the date line looks small and thin in the contact sheet. The guide asks for every non-Chinese line to be at least 9.5 units (30 px) tall. The brief's 10-unit font size only yields about 20 px glyphs.

Suggested fix: Raise the English and date lines to about 15-16 units of font size, so caps and digits are at least 30 px at zoom 1.1. Either widen the right reserve inside the 270-unit limit by narrowing the Chinese reserve slightly, or stack the date on its own row and make the panel taller (y 474-524).

### [should-fix] Top-view pelican reads as a dead or flattened bird, not a rider seen from above

Evidence: Top-view crop at 50.02 and the 48.0-48.375 run: there is no neck, and the head is a white disc glued to the body oval. The thighs are long flat pink bars sticking out behind. The handlebar grips and pedals are small red and gold bits scattered round the body. The bicycle line is mostly hidden under the body. At film scale it looks like a bird lying on the water, and the pedalling in step is barely visible, so the bar-2 joke depends on a shape that does not read.

Suggested fix: Show the bicycle's full line clearly: tyres as dark bands ahead of and behind the body, a handlebar bar across in front of the chest, and a short visible neck and head with the bill extended over the front wheel. Make the feet and pedals larger and alternate them clearly left and right with the crank. Drop the pink thigh bars, or shorten them to stubs under the wings.

### [should-fix] Astonishment event is too small to read at film scale

Evidence: In the contact sheet, frames 49.50 versus 50.00 look almost identical. Even at full size (49.97 versus 50.02) the change is a 10-15 px finger on faces about 30 px tall, and no figure changes silhouette. The lute player is off screen from u = 3, so 'the musicians stop' is not visible for him.

Suggested fix: Make the state change visible in silhouette: turn every head clearly toward the pelican, keep the raised forearm and hand in a strong contrasting skin tone against the robe, and have the lute player's lute lowered or laid on the carpet. Consider placing at least one musician so it is on screen at u = 3.

### [should-fix] Title panel reads as a modern signboard rather than an illuminated cartouche

Evidence: Title crop at 50.02: two plain cream rectangles with wavy gold edges sit on a lapis strip with star dots. The brief asks for 'words in reserve shapes outlined with gold cloud scrolls'. There are no cloud-band (chi) outlines around the words and no gold ground, so it looks like a shop sign or label.

Suggested fix: Set the words on a gold ground inside cloud-band reserves, so the cream shapes follow the word outlines with lobed contours and a fine gold cloud-scroll border. Put small floral illumination in the gaps, and remove the flat rectangular look.

### [should-fix] Gallery still: pelican's bill stabs into the arch figure's face

Evidence: Still (u = 3.3): the yellow bill runs straight into the pink-robed woman's head in the first arch, hiding her finger-on-lips gesture, which is the subject of the still. The Chinese title is also cropped out (only the English reserve shows).

Suggested fix: Shift the still's cx by about 40-60 units left (or set u to about 3.1) so the bill ends in the lapis gap before the arch and her gesture reads. Optionally choose a cx that keeps both reserves of the title visible.

### [minor] Title is in full view for barely 2 s

Evidence: 47.75 (u = 0.75): the right edge of the English reserve is at screen x of about 1910, and it first becomes fully visible here. 49.8 (u = 2.8): the left edge of the Chinese reserve is at x of about 0. That is about 2.05 s of full view, which only just clears the minimum.

Suggested fix: Shift the panel 10-20 units right, or narrow it slightly, to gain margin.

### [minor] Sticker halo around the side-view bicycle; craquelure and paper barely visible

Evidence: Pedal run crops at 48.0-48.375 and 50.02: a pale rim outlines the wheels against the lawn, like a cut-out. The brief's faint craquelure and gold rubbing are visible only in the title panel.

Suggested fix: Remove or darken the halo around the patch. Add a very faint global craquelure and gold-rubbing pass over the baked page.

## Items of the brief's checklist that failed

- **Top view is clearly the same pelican seen from above, pedalling in step**: Crop at 50.02 and the 48.0-48.375 run: the top view sits at the same x as the side view, has the white body, black tips, tail fan and gold bill, and its pink feet and pedals shift between frames. But it has no visible neck (the head sits straight on the body oval). The pink thigh bars, red grips and gold struts clutter it, and at a glance it reads as a flattened bird floating on the water rather than a rider seen from above. The pedalling is barely perceptible.
- **Title legible, three parts**: All three parts are present and legible (zoomed crop at 50.02). Measured at 1080p: the Chinese glyphs are about 61 px tall (pass). The English cap M is about 21 px (38 px from ascender to descender), and the date digits '1600' are about 20 px tall. Those lines are well under the guide's 30 px if the guide means glyph height.
