## Section 3 · han (film 17–23 s)

- **Ride:** Standard pace for the whole section. han.js exports no motion, camera, road or poseHz, so x = 166.67·u and the pelican completes 6.000 pedal turns by u = 6. The pelican's pose is smooth. The procession is stepped: gaitStep = floor(u·12) + phase, mod 6 (han.js:429; STEPS = 6, STRIDE = 0.5), so the horses and carriage horses change sprite at 12 Hz, six steps per 0.5 s stride. In each trot stride the near diagonal pair lands at gait step 0 and the far pair at step 3 (FORE/HIND tables, han.js:189-204; the far legs use (st+3)%6), so each horse has two footfalls per beat, 0.25 s apart. BOB and RISE also repeat every 3 steps (0.25 s). A member's footfall times are u = ((3 - phase) mod 3)/12 + 0.25k. Lance outriders (phase 0 and 3): on the beat and the off-eighth, u = 0, 0.25, 0.5, ..., but they sit beyond the right edge of the screen most of the time. 功曹车 carriage horse (phase 2): u = 0.083 + 0.25k. 主簿车 carriage horse and the near attendant (phase 4, both 4 mod 3 = 1): u = 0.167 + 0.25k. Far attendant (phase 1): u = 0.167 + 0.25k. So none of the visible horses lands on a beat; the ones on screen land 0.083 or 0.167 s after each quarter-beat grid point. The carriage wheels rotate smoothly at the true rate (angle = x/30, han.js:837). They are not stepped.
- **Pelican:** A white chiselled line drawing cut into an inked stone rubbing, at the same scale as the procession. It is an outline puppet with spoked wheels and is seen whole, so its pedalling is visible. It rides between two carriages.
- **What could sound:** An ink rubbing of a carved stone. A horse-and-carriage procession: two lance-carrying outriders (mostly off screen), a light carriage with a big spoked wheel and a parasol, a second carriage with a canopy, and two mounted attendants. The horses trot and the wheels turn. An ink pad dabs the paper. Fixed in the stone: a feast with seated figures holding cups, and a frieze of a galloping tiger, deer, hare and bird. Cracked stone, paper margin, one red seal.
- **Differs from the brief:** The tap times 2.0/2.5/3.0/3.5 agree. The pad appears 0.034 s before each tap, as the As built section says. The brief's sound table says 'hoofbeats every 0.5', but the code is a two-footfall trot: each horse lands twice per 0.5 s stride (gait steps 0 and 3), with per-member phase offsets. The visible carriage horses and attendants land at u ≡ 0.083 / 0.167 (mod 0.25), off the beat. Only the lance outriders, mostly off screen, land on the beat grid.
